Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

What the app does

The Scam Pause application is an internal publishing tool. It uses the YouTube Data API v3 only to upload videos to the Scam Pause YouTube channel owned by Scam Pause.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from the public. The app does not offer sign-in to the public and does not request access to anyone else's Google account.

Google user data

The only Google account the app accesses is our own channel account, and only to upload and manage our own videos. OAuth tokens are kept privately by Scam Pause and are not shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party platforms

When you watch our videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
